# WasuliBhai Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

WasuliBhai helps you keep track of your subscriptions. It is built to work without collecting any of your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** WasuliBhai has no account, no server and no analytics. The subscriptions and settings you enter are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

## Data on your device

- **Subscriptions and settings** stay in the app's storage on your phone. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include them if you have backups turned on.
- **Clipboard:** the app reads your clipboard only when you tap "Paste from clipboard" on the Find subscriptions screen. The text is processed on your device and is not saved or sent anywhere.
- **Pasted SMS or email text** is read on your device to suggest a subscription. Only the subscription you choose to save is stored.
- **Notifications:** renewal reminders are scheduled on your device. No push notification service is used.
- **Export:** if you use "Export as JSON", the file goes wherever you choose to share it.

## Links to other apps

The "App Store subscriptions" and "Google Play subscriptions" buttons open Apple's or Google's own pages. Their privacy policies apply there.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Delete all subscriptions**, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored on your device.

## Children

WasuliBhai is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
